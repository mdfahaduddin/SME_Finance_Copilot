About 50 seconds. The copilot does four jobs that map directly to Topic E. It analyses the business with an explainable health score. It forecasts cash thirteen weeks ahead, with an uncertainty band, and alerts with a concrete next step. It matches financing across a lender marketplace by true cost, fit, approval odds, affordability and speed, and explains why other products are excluded. And it acts: it can draft a payment reminder, schedule a supplier payment, ask a supplier for more time or prepare a loan application. Every one of those goes into a queue that a person approves.
