SME Finance Copilot

An AI agent that analyses a small business, sees cash trouble coming, finds the right financing, and acts only with the owner's approval.

FinSight AI · Md. Fahad Uddin · Md Ashiqur Rahman · Biprajit Karmakar
Noakhali Science and Technology University · Bangladesh University of Business and Technology

Opening, about 30 seconds. Small-business owners make cash decisions every week without a finance team. We built SME Finance Copilot: an AI agent that participates in the business's finances. It analyses the books, forecasts cash, finds financing and takes actions, but only when a human approves. The line behind me is the real forecast shape from our demo business: a sharp dip six weeks out that the copilot catches before it happens.
