About 45 seconds. Three pains we designed for. First, cash surprises: a late customer plus a big bill in the same week, and the owner only finds out when the payment bounces. Second, financing fog: many products, unclear eligibility, and the quickest money is usually the most expensive; in our sandbox marketplace the merchant cash advance works out above fifty percent a year. Third, the numbers nobody reads: margins, how fast customers pay, how much debt the business can carry. A generic chatbot can talk about all this, but it guesses numbers and cannot be trusted to act. That is the gap.
