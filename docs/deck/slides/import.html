About 45 seconds. A copilot is only as good as its data, so any business can sign up and bring its own. Owners download a statement from their bank as PDF, CSV or Excel, and we read it. For PDFs we check every row against the running balance printed on the statement, so a misread figure cannot slip in. Scanned statements are read with OCR on our own server, and here we are strict: only rows that the balance confirms are imported, and if too much of a scan is uncertain we refuse it rather than guess. And the whole product works in the business's own currency: a Bangladeshi shop sees lender criteria, approval limits and the copilot's answers in taka. In our evidence run a new business in BDT imported a two-page statement, all five rows reconciled, and importing it again added nothing.
