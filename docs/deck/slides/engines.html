About 60 seconds. Five deterministic engines. The health score is six transparent sub-scores: liquidity, profitability, growth, collections, debt capacity and stability, each with its weight and explanation visible in the app. The forecast is a hybrid: open invoices arrive on the due date plus that customer's usual delay, bills on their due dates, recurring payments detected from the ledger, and retail sales from exponential smoothing times last year's seasonal pattern, with a P10 to P90 band. Alerts come from nine rules, each naming what to do. Financing filters on eligibility, then scores cost, fit, approval odds, affordability and speed. Scenarios reuse the forecast pipeline, so what-if numbers are consistent with the base case.
