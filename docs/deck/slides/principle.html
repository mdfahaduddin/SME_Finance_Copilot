The engines calculate.

8/8 figures verified

Every number in an answer is checked against the tool results before you see it.

8 / 13

About 30 seconds. This is the core idea. The model never does arithmetic on your money. It decides which engines to call and explains what they return. After it answers, a groundedness check parses every money figure and percentage, in any currency, and matches it against the tool results. The badge, eight out of eight figures verified, came from the live answer to "can I afford a forty-five-thousand-dollar oven" in our evidence run. If a figure cannot be traced, the badge lists it instead of hiding it.
